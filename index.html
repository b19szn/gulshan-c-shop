Our Story

Gulshan Coffee began as a small neighborhood roastery with a big dream: to connect people through remarkable coffee. We source ethically grown beans, roast in small batches, and craft beverages that celebrate diversity of flavor—every cup tells a story.

Our space is designed for lingering conversations, quiet study, and spontaneous meetings. We brew with intention, keep the atmosphere warm, and serve with a smile.
